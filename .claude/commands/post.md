Write a post on `$ARGUMENTS`. With no argument, propose three themes first.

The procedure is [`docs/writer/writer.md`](../../docs/writer/writer.md) and the
style it writes to is [`docs/writer/style.md`](../../docs/writer/style.md).
Read both in full and follow them; this file only starts it. They live under
`docs/` for the reason `docs/reader/loop.md` gives: that is the half of the
repository a factory release does not rewrite.

It runs here, in the session, and fetches nothing. It never publishes and
never merges a post until the author says it is ready.
