Write this week's installment on `$ARGUMENTS`: one Substack issue and 2-3
LinkedIn posts cut from it, from one interview. With no argument, propose three
themes first.

The procedure is [`docs/writer/writer.md`](../../docs/writer/writer.md), the
style it writes to is [`docs/writer/style.md`](../../docs/writer/style.md), and
the series it is an installment of is a file under
[`docs/writer/series/`](../../docs/writer/series/) - named by the theme or the
author, never assumed. Read all three in full and follow them; this file only
starts it. They live under `docs/` for the reason `docs/reader/loop.md` gives:
that is the half of the repository a factory release does not rewrite.

It runs here, in the session, and fetches nothing. It never publishes and
never merges a piece until the author says it is ready.
