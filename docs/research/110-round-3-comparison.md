## Headline

1. **Best quality per dollar: the $400-$695 tier - and only because of one
   pick, M1, the Jamie Haller Penny Loafer at $625.** It is the only pick
   whose brand is "worth investing in". That verdict rests on one
   multi-year wear test of a sibling colourway, and the black pick itself
   has **thin** evidence. The claim is narrow, and "Ending" says how narrow.
2. **Only one pick is a buy: U1, the Madewell Greta at $98** - the only
   pick with evidence stronger than thin, carried with its most-repeated
   complaint (leather or lining failing early, 4 sources).
3. **The over-$695 tier is a pass on both picks.** Both brands are "skip"
   in #113, and neither exact shoe has a creator, editor or long-term item.
4. **Three picks are "try in store first"** (both MARGAUX shoes and M1):
   thin evidence, but a brand verdict that does not rule them out and a
   named complaint a try-on can settle.
5. **Of the wishlist tier's brands, one earns its price on this evidence -
   Jamie Haller.** BY FAR and Dear Frances are "fine for the price" at
   about $695, which is not shown to be earned.

## Ending: which tier gives the best quality per dollar

**The $400-$695 tier, on the strength of M1 alone.** The Jamie Haller Penny
Loafer at $625 is the only pick from a brand rated **worth investing in**:
a named publication's multi-year wear test with the shoe ageing well and
resoled, and trade press on where it is made
([#113 Jamie Haller](110-social-proof-top-tier-and-brand-verdicts.md#jamie-haller)).
It fits all four Style lines. The other mid-tier pick, M2, is a pass, so
this is not a tier average - it is one shoe.

**Why not under $400.** It is the tier with the only buy (U1) and the only
evidence stronger than thin. But every brand in it is **fine for the
price**, not worth investing in: the Greta's evidence is mostly about its
leather and lining failing early, and MARGAUX's one long test reports a
flimsier sole on later pairs
([#113 MARGAUX](110-social-proof-top-tier-and-brand-verdicts.md#margaux)).
MARGAUX's James at $375 is the closest rival to M1: the same silhouette,
also sacchetto, also resoled on record. It costs $250 less and rates one
step lower. **If the owner values price over a verdict resting on one
test, under $400 is the answer**, and the James is the shoe.

**Why not over $695.** Both brands are **skip**, both picks are thin, and
O1 has no evidence at all
([#113 Recommendation](110-social-proof-top-tier-and-brand-verdicts.md#recommendation)).
Spending more here buys less evidence, not more. #112 and #113 both found
the same pattern: the more a shoe costs, the less anyone has said about it.

**What is thin, and how it limits the claim.**

- **M1's own evidence is thin: 0 exact items.** The verdict that carries
  the tier is a *brand* verdict. It rests on one test, which is uncritical,
  affiliate, from 2024, of the Oxblood colourway, and predates the brand's
  2025-26 expansion into bags, jewellery and sneakers. If that test does
  not transfer to black buffalo, the $400-$695 tier has no evidence for
  its best pick, and the claim falls back to under $400.
- **Six of seven picks are thin.** The per-pick verdicts lean on brand
  verdicts, and **seven of the ten brand verdicts rest on no reputable
  test** (#113 headline). Every judgement here is about what has been
  published, not about which shoe is better made.
- **No construction teardown exists for any brand** (#113 "Searches that
  came back empty"), so "quality" throughout means wear reports and
  materials, not construction.
- **One retailer shaped the tiers**: six of seven links are nordstrom.com
  ([#111 Recommendation](110-round-3-picks.md#recommendation)), so "best in
  a tier" means best of what Nordstrom and Shopbop list.

**Strongest argument against, looked for in the sources:** *"The tier with
the best quality per dollar is the one where you can actually check the
quality, and that is under $400."* It is a fair reading of #112's own
recommendation, which names U1 as the only pick with enough evidence to
judge. The answer here still names $400-$695 because the question is
quality per dollar, and the only evidence of quality lasting years - a
resoled, multi-year wear - is Jamie Haller's and MARGAUX's, and #113 rated
Jamie Haller higher. The Greta's evidence is volume, mostly affiliate, and
its most repeated finding is a durability failure.

**What would flip it:**

- **To under $400:** the owner deciding the Oxblood test does not transfer
  to black; a sizing or durability pattern on post-2025 Jamie Haller pairs
  (#113's flip for that brand); or a wear report on the black M1 that goes
  badly.
- **Towards M1 as a buy:** the owner deciding colour is immaterial for this
  line, plus a try-on that settles the sizing.
- **To over $695:** a wear report past a season on the Fay, or a cobbler
  confirming its sole can be replaced (#113's flip for The Row).
